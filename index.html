Privacy Policy

Quran Karim (Қурони Карим)

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Quran Karim does not collect personal data and does not send it to anyone.

This policy covers the “Қурони Карим” (Quran Karim) Android app on Google Play, published by Sulton UzDev.

Data we don't collect

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data over the internet.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores the following on your phone so it can work. We can't see this data.

  • Settings: theme, text sizes, script, translation and notes display, reading mode
  • Your reading position and khatm progress
  • Whether the reminder is on, and its time

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the daily reminder. The reminder is optional, and the rest of the app works if you decline.
  • Run at startup: to restore the reminder after your phone restarts.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, your phone may include the app's settings and reading position in the backup in your own Google account. Google manages this, and we can't see that copy. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Google Play

When you download the app from Google Play, Google handles data under its own privacy policy. Google Play may show developers statistics about the app.

Deleting your data

You can reset khatm progress in the app's settings. To delete everything, clear the app's data in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date above.
